## Who we are
BizPulse AI ("we") provides AI-powered business intelligence software. Contact:
<support email>. Data controller: <legal entity, address>.

## What we collect
- **Account data** — your email address and a password hash (we never store the
  plain password).
- **Business data** — the figures you enter or upload (revenue, expenses, cash,
  and any business details you provide) so we can compute metrics and analyses.
- **Usage data** — counts of analyses, reports and uploads, used to apply your
  plan's limits.
- **Technical data** — minimal logs needed to operate and secure the service.

## Where it is stored
Account and business data are stored in our Supabase (Postgres) database, protected
by row-level security so that only members of your organization can read your rows.
Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth.

## AI processing
When you run an analysis, the relevant figures are sent to our AI provider through a
server-side function to generate written commentary. The AI provider does not receive
your password. We do not use your business data to train third-party models unless
you explicitly opt in.

## Payments
Card and UPI details are handled by our payment processor (Razorpay / Stripe). We do
not store full payment instrument details on our servers.

## Your rights
You can access, export, correct or delete your data. Deleting your account removes
your organization's data, subject to legal retention requirements. To exercise these
rights, contact <support email>.
